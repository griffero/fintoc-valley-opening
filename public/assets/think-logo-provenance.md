# think coffee logo

`think-logo.png` is an unchanged copy of the image supplied by the user on 2026-09-07, named `Screenshot 2026-09-07 at 11.14.40 PM.png`.

At the user's request, `think-logo.svg` traces this PNG with Potrace 1.16. It contains eight filled paths (two brain halves and six lettering components), a transparent background and a 251 × 241 viewBox. It has no embedded bitmap or font dependency. The trace was visually compared with the supplied PNG.

The SVG paths are extruded into shallow 3D geometry on the coffee stand's sign. Their native proportions, letterforms and brain openings are retained. The PNG is kept as the unmodified source reference.
